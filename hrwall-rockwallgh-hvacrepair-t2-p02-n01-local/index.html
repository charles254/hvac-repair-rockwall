Thermal & Airflow Warning Signs

When conditioned air loses its crisp chill or airflow drops noticeably from your ceiling registers, internal mechanical or airflow restrictions are actively choking system efficiency.

  • Supply air temperature differential (split) dropping below the expected 16-20°F range
  • Rooms on the sunny south or west side of the house failing to cool during late afternoons
  • Blower motor running continuously while indoor temperatures continue creeping upward
  • Air filters coating with dense dust and debris within two weeks of replacement

Electrical & Mechanical Distress Indicators

Electrical components inside your outdoor condenser and indoor air handler produce clear warning indicators when operating under excessive friction, thermal heat, or voltage irregularities.

  • Metallic screeching or grinding sounds indicating worn blower motor or condenser fan bearings
  • Rapid short-cycling where the compressor starts and shuts down every two to five minutes
  • Frequent circuit breaker trips at the main electrical panel during compressor startup
  • Musty, damp odors from vents signaling microbial growth inside the evaporator drip pan

Frequently asked questions

Why is my AC running for hours without reaching the thermostat setpoint?

Continuous running without reaching setpoint usually points to low refrigerant from a slow coil leak, dirty outdoor condenser coils, an undersized system, or leaky attic ductwork pulling 140°F attic air into your living spaces.

What causes ice on the outdoor copper pipes in the middle of summer?

Ice on refrigerant lines is typically caused by severely restricted airflow (such as a choked air filter or blocked return grille) or low refrigerant levels that drop evaporator temperatures below freezing.
